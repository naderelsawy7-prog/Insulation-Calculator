Calculation basis, assumptions & limitations

Thermal model & report reference

  • Heat loss and energy cost: estimates external convection and radiation heat loss, annual operating hours, fuel cost per MMBtu, and fuel-to-useful-heat efficiency. The simplified screening calculation assumes hot service and steady-state inputs, with process temperature above surface temperature above ambient.
  • Potential savings: for Not Installed, Removed, or Damaged items, the default assumption is that post-insulation heat loss is 30% of current loss (70% savings). This is editable, but it is not a proposed insulation design or guaranteed saving.
  • CAPEX: the default screening estimate is surface area × $150/m². The rate is editable and is not a quotation or validated estimate. Payback and annual simple ROI use this area-based CAPEX proxy.
  • Reference conductivity: uses the embedded material table and nearest lower temperature band (0, 100, 200, 400, 600°C). Verify temperature applicability and conductivity with the selected product supplier, especially above the highest tabulated band.
  • Financial basis: energy-only NPV discounts annual energy savings using the entered project life and discount rate. Carbon-inclusive value is a separate shadow-price case, not revenue, tax, credit, or cash saving. Annual simple ROI is not IRR; taxes, escalation, FX, and detailed cash-flow timing are not modelled.
  • Emissions: the default factor of 0.181 kg CO₂/kWh-fuel is editable. Confirm its source, year, gas basis, and reporting boundary. Avoided emissions are estimates until measured and reconciled under an agreed M&V method.
  • ISO reference: ISO 12241:2022, Thermal insulation for building equipment and industrial installations — Calculation rules, is listed as a reference for predominantly steady-state heat-transfer calculations. This screening tool has not been independently verified as conforming to the full standard; confirm the licensed standard, project conditions, and any applicable client/site requirements before design or approval. Official ISO listing.
  • Safety & operations: surface-temperature, moisture, risk, and priority labels are screening flags only—not safety determinations or standards-compliance checks. Field-check data, assess operating cases, maintainability, safety, environmental constraints, controls, utilities, interfaces, and online/turnaround execution. Follow site approval and MOC processes.
  • Applicability: the cost model is fuel-gas/hot-service based. Cold or cryogenic service, cooling/refrigeration economics, and detailed retrofit design require a different model and additional inputs.

Interpretation notes

Energy-only economics are shown separately from carbon value. The carbon-inclusive case applies the internal shadow price only to estimated avoided emissions; it is not cash revenue. Confirm the baseline, M&V method, and cost basis before using benefits for approval or reporting.
Risk and surface-temperature categories are screening flags only; they are not safety determinations or standards-compliance checks.
Calculation basis & ISO reference notes

Report interpretation

  • Potential savings use the editable residual-loss assumption (default 30%). CAPEX uses surface area × the editable rate (default $150/m²) for items with an action candidate.
  • Energy-only NPV discounts annual energy savings using the selected life and discount rate. Carbon-inclusive NPV adds a separate shadow value for estimated avoided emissions.
  • Annual simple ROI is annual energy savings divided by estimated CAPEX; it is not IRR. Tax, escalation, FX, and detailed cash-flow timing are not included.
  • The report references ISO 12241:2022 but does not claim certification or verified conformity. Check the method and operating/design conditions against the applicable licensed standard and project requirements.
  • No operating change, insulation design, safety approval, or external emissions claim should be based solely on this calculator.
